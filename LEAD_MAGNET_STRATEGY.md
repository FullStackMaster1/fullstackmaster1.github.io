# Lead Magnet Strategy - Free Guides as Honey Spots

## 🎯 Goal
Transform "Free Guides" into high-converting lead magnets that capture emails and convert visitors into coaching clients.

## 💡 Innovative Ideas

### 1. **Interactive Role Assessment Quiz** (Highest Conversion)
**Concept**: "Find Your Path to Director/VP" - Interactive quiz that:
- Asks 5-7 questions about current role, experience, target company
- Provides personalized roadmap based on answers
- **Email gate** before showing results
- Shows skill gaps and next steps
- Offers "Get Personalized Coaching Plan" CTA

**Why it works**: 
- Interactive = higher engagement
- Personalized = more valuable
- Email gate = lead capture
- Creates urgency ("See your personalized roadmap")

### 2. **Email-Gated Comprehensive Roadmaps** (Classic but Effective)
**Concept**: Full PDF roadmaps for each role:
- "Director of Engineering Roadmap" (90-day plan)
- "Senior Director Roadmap" (6-month plan)
- "Senior Product Manager Roadmap" (FAANG-specific)
- "Senior Program Manager Roadmap" (AWS/Google/Microsoft)
- "Senior Technical Program Manager Roadmap" (L6-L7)

**What makes it special**:
- Include salary ranges, timeline, skill checklist
- Company-specific sections (AWS vs Google vs Microsoft)
- Real interview questions for each role
- Success stories from that role level

### 3. **Visual Career Progression Map** (Engaging)
**Concept**: Interactive visual showing:
- Current role → Target role progression
- Skills needed at each level
- Timeline estimates
- Salary progression
- Click to expand each role for details

**Why it works**:
- Visual = easy to understand
- Shows clear path forward
- Creates aspiration
- Shareable (social proof)

### 4. **Skill Gap Analysis Tool** (High Value)
**Concept**: "Are You Ready for Director/VP?" assessment:
- Rate yourself on 20 key skills
- Get instant gap analysis
- See what you're missing
- Get personalized learning path
- **Email required** to download full report

**Why it works**:
- Self-assessment = engagement
- Reveals blind spots = creates need
- Personalized = high value
- Natural coaching CTA

### 5. **Company-Specific Roadmaps** (Targeted)
**Concept**: Separate roadmaps for:
- "AWS Director Interview Roadmap"
- "Google L7 Interview Roadmap"
- "Microsoft Principal Engineer Roadmap"
- "Meta Director Roadmap"

**Why it works**:
- Highly targeted = better SEO
- Shows insider knowledge
- More specific = more valuable
- Targets people actively interviewing

### 6. **Salary Progression Calculator** (Viral Potential)
**Concept**: Interactive tool showing:
- Current salary → Target salary
- Role progression impact
- Company-specific ranges
- Timeline to reach target
- **Email to download full salary guide**

**Why it works**:
- Money = high interest
- Interactive = engaging
- Shareable results
- Creates FOMO

### 7. **Role Comparison Tool** (Decision Support)
**Concept**: "Director vs Senior Director vs VP" comparison:
- Side-by-side comparison
- Skills required
- Interview differences
- Salary ranges
- Which is right for you?
- **Email for detailed comparison PDF**

### 8. **90-Day Action Plan Templates** (Immediate Value)
**Concept**: Downloadable templates:
- "90-Day Director Prep Plan"
- "90-Day VP Prep Plan"
- "90-Day Senior PM Prep Plan"
- Week-by-week breakdown
- Daily action items
- **Email-gated PDF**

## 📧 Email Capture Strategy

### Before Roadmap Access:
- "Get your personalized roadmap"
- "Download the complete guide"
- "See your skill gap analysis"

### After Email Capture:
- Send roadmap PDF immediately
- Follow-up email sequence (3-5 emails)
- Offer free consultation
- Share success stories
